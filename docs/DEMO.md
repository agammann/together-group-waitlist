# Together demo

Show a couple moving from a flexible waitlist request to a confirmed ceramics booking in under three minutes.

## Before recording

Run the app and Ollama, check that the selected model is available, and decide which checkout path the recording will show. A PayPal integration claim requires actual sandbox buyer approval and a server-verified capture. Label a simulated checkout clearly. Use synthetic customer details, keep credentials out of the recording, and reset demo state only when no payment is in progress.

Check the six displayed dates before speaking. In a fresh schedule, each session has one open place. Pick two or three acceptable sessions and a budget that covers two seats in at least one of them. A two-person group must remain waiting while only one place is available. Use the actual displayed dates in the request so the interpretation can be visibly checked.

## Recording sequence

| Time | On screen | Narration goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Customer screen and six-session schedule | “Two friends want a pottery class together. Several dates work, but joining one date at a time makes them repeat their preferences.” |
| 0:20–0:50 | Plain-language request and reviewed proposal | State two people, acceptable dates, and a total budget. Show that local Qwen proposes structured preferences and the customer reviews them before joining. |
| 0:50–1:15 | Studio screen shows one open place, then releases one existing place | Show the two-person group still waiting with only one open place. Release one existing place in an acceptable session; the resulting two open places become a single offer for the whole group. |
| 1:15–1:40 | Customer offer and countdown | Show the held seat count, session, total cost, and ten-minute deadline. Other availability includes the held seats. |
| 1:40–2:25 | Customer approval and PayPal sandbox checkout | Show the customer approving this booking, PayPal buyer approval, return, and the server-confirmed result. Avoid implying that a callback alone proves payment. |
| 2:25–2:45 | Receipt and studio schedule | Show one reservation for the complete group, a verified sandbox capture reference, and capacity consistent with the reservation. |
| 2:45–2:55 | App overview | “One studio, six sessions, one timezone. Local Qwen interprets preferences; the booking engine enforces dates, budget, and seat limits.” |

If sandbox approval takes longer than the recording budget, shorten earlier scenes. Do not replace the actual approval and confirmation with a static receipt while claiming to demonstrate real checkout.

## Optional follow-up demonstration

Create an offer and let it expire. Show the expiry status and released capacity, then a later eligible offer. Demonstrate a date outside the selection and a price above budget being rejected. Keep these in a separate recording or shorten the main sequence; the contest video should stay under three minutes.

## Delivery checklist

Record a visible interaction with the running application. Use original visual assets and omit unlicensed music. Watch the exported video once for readable text, credentials, correct payment labeling, and consistency with the current build. The contest needs a publicly visible YouTube link; publication and contest submission are separate actions still to be authorized.
